    № 03How it works

    Three steps.One transaction.

    FIAT is a pump.fun launcher. You keep your creator fees, FIAT takes nothing, and the currency pairing goes on-chain with the coin.

    I

    Pick a currency

    47 to choose from: the lira, the peso, the naira, the yen, the krona. Its live rate becomes your coin's other half.

    II

    Print your coin

    Name, ticker, a picture (or let FIAT print you a banknote) and an optional first buy.

    III

    Sign one transaction

    Your wallet signs. The coin is created on pump.fun with the pairing written into the same transaction.

    TRANSACTION RECEIPTFIAT · SOLANA MAINNET
    // what your launch transaction contains
1  pump.fun  create_v2     name, ticker, metadata
2  pump.fun  buy           your first buy (optional)
3  memo      "fiat:v1:TRY:LIRA"   ← the pairing
4  system    tag           registry …
// signed by: your wallet + the new mint. nothing else.
    FIAT FEE 0.00 SOL
    SIGNED
    0%
    FIAT takes no fee. You only pay pump.fun's normal fees and the network fee. Creator fees stay yours.
    ⛓
    The pairing is public. Every FIAT launch carries a memo and lists the FIAT registry, so anyone can find all of them on-chain.
    ¤
    No keys, no custody. FIAT builds the transaction, your wallet signs it. The registry is a program address nobody controls.

    № 06FAQ

    Questions.Answered in plain words.

    What does "pairing" a coin with a currency mean?

    Your coin is a normal pump.fun coin. The pairing is a label written into its launch transaction as a memo (fiat:v1:TRY:LIRA). FIAT reads it from the chain and shows your coin next to that currency's exchange rate. It doesn't peg, back or link the coin's price to the currency.

    Where do the exchange rates come from?

    Daily reference rates come from the open fawazahmed0 currency-api (published daily for 200+ currencies). Where an intraday quote is available, the board marks the row LIVE. Everything else is labelled DAILY with its date. Nothing is estimated or invented.

    What is "the melt"?

    A live projection: $100 held in a currency, moving at that currency's own pace over the last year, counted from the moment you opened the page. It uses the real 1-year change from the board. It is not a forecast.

    What does a launch cost?

    FIAT takes no fee. pump.fun charges its normal fees, the network charges a small fee, and your first buy (optional) is whatever you choose. Have a little extra SOL, about 0.03, for rent and fees.

    Who gets the creator fees?

    You do. The wallet that launches the coin is its creator on pump.fun, exactly like launching on pump.fun directly.

    Can FIAT touch my wallet or my coin?

    No. FIAT builds the transaction and simulates it, and your wallet signs it. The new mint's key is made in your browser, signs once, and is discarded. FIAT never holds keys or funds.

    Why are launches paused?

    Launches open when the official $FIAT token is out, so nobody can pass off a launch as ours before then. The CA will only be posted here and on our X.

    How do I find every FIAT coin myself?

    Every launch lists the FIAT registry address as a read-only account. Look it up on any Solana explorer, or call getSignaturesForAddress on it: each transaction is one launch, and its memo says which currency.

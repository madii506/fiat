    FAQ

    Questions.

    What does pairing mean?

    A label in your coin's launch transaction (fiat:v1:TRY:LIRA). FIAT shows your coin next to that currency. It's not a peg.

    Where do the rates come from?

    Daily reference rates from the open currency-api. Rows marked LIVE use an intraday quote.

    What does it cost?

    FIAT takes 0%. You pay pump.fun's normal fees, plus your first buy if you want one.

    Who gets the creator fees?

    You. Your wallet is the creator on pump.fun.

    Can FIAT touch my funds?

    No. Your wallet signs. FIAT never holds keys or funds.
